## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, warm, and tropical-rustic gastronomic experience situated in Colmenar Viejo, Madrid. It merges the atmospheric comfort of an evening lounge with the vibrant cultural energy of the Caribbean coast and Venezuelan heritage. 

The aesthetic centers around an immersive, high-contrast dark theme influenced by kinetic art—most notably the chromatic rhythm of Carlos Cruz-Diez’s iconic Maiquetía airport floor installations. Deep twilight and carbon surfaces provide a grounded backdrop, allowing solar yellow, rich maritime blue, and energetic crimson to punctuate navigation, CTAs, and interactive feedback. 

Translucent glassmorphic panels, fine luminous hairline borders, and polished typographic contrast deliver an editorial feel that invites guests to explore menus, reserve tables, and engage with the bar's culinary narrative without cognitive fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by extreme dark foundations that replicate the warm intimacy of a modern evening bar.

- **Primary (`#FFCC00`)**: Solar Yellow. Applied to key calls-to-action, active category states, pricing indicators, and luminous focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#0033A0` / `#00247D`)**: Royal Caribbean Blue. Used for contextual badges, structural contrast ribbons, and secondary state surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#CF142B` / `#E62035`)**: Crimson Red. Reserved for seasonal tags, alerts, badges ("Plato Estrella"), and kinetic gradient stops.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering**:
  - Base Canvas: `#080A0F` (Night Sky)
  - Surface Raised: `#121620` (Deep Shadow)
  - Surface Glass: `rgba(24, 30, 44, 0.72)` (Caribbean Night Glass)
  - Glass Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Kinetic Gradient (Cruz-Diez accent): Linear gradients transitioning sequentially across `#FFCC00`, `#0033A0`, and `#CF142B` at 1px thickness along card edges or divider rules.
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - Text Primary: `#F8FAFC`
  - Text Secondary: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.72)`
  - Text Muted: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.45)`

## Typography

Typography establishes an approachable yet distinctly contemporary identity. 

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Soft terminal curves paired with geometric strength. High weights (700 and 800) give hero titles and menu categories punchy editorial authority, keeping the vibe confident and welcoming.
- **Body & Editorial Content (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: A clean, humanist sans-serif providing high legibility on dark glass backgrounds. It preserves character distinctions across multilingual menus (Spanish/English) and provides relaxed reading rhythm in ingredient lists and event descriptions.
- **Uppercase micro-labels**: Applied to dietary filters, rum specs, and operational status (e.g., "ABIERTO HOY"), rendered in `label-sm` with letter spacing to balance visual weight against deep shadows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a fluid 12-column grid system on desktop screens transitioning down to a 4-column system on mobile hand-held views.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (Margin: `1.25rem`, Gutter: `1rem`)
  - Tablet: `768px – 1024px` (Margin: `2rem`, Gutter: `1.25rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (Margin: `3rem`, Gutter: `1.5rem`, max container constraint: `1320px`)
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence**:
  - Component internals strictly adhere to 4px/8px modular jumps (`space-xs` to `space-md`).
  - Menu cards maintain generous internal padding (`space-lg`) to preserve isolation between culinary photos, dietary badges, and price indicators.
  - Section separations leverage `space-xl` scaled dynamically (2.5rem to 4.5rem) to ensure distinct rhythmic pauses between tapas, main courses, cocktails, and reservations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh drop shadows; instead, it uses layered luminous translucency, backdrop diffusion, and ambient glow.

- **Canvas Tier (0dp)**: Flat `#080A0F` base with subtle ambient color radial blurs (`rgba(255, 204, 0, 0.04)` and `rgba(0, 51, 160, 0.05)`) placed strategically in viewport corners to simulate warm stage lighting.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Low cards & list rows)**: Background `rgba(18, 22, 32, 0.85)` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Blur radius: `12px`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Interactive cards, active tabs, modals)**: Background `rgba(24, 30, 44, 0.75)` with a 1px perimeter border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Blur radius: `20px`.
- **Active Focus & Hover**: Ambient solar glow using tinted drop shadows (`0 8px 30px rgba(255, 204, 0, 0.18)`), elevating the component forward and indicating tactile readiness.
- **Cruz-Diez Kinetic Accent**: Key elevated modules (like the live table booking widget or featured rum tasting card) incorporate a multi-stop 1px border gradient transitioning from `#FFCC00` through `#0033A0` to `#CF142B` with a gentle `0 0 16px rgba(255, 204, 0, 0.15)` bleed.

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of `2`, the shape framework balances friendly hospitality with modern architectural precision:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Inputs, small tag badges, stepper buttons, and icon containers.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px)**: Gastronomic menu item cards, filter bars, cocktail highlight panels, and QR code wrappers.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px)**: Major interaction blocks, modal dialogs, promotional hero banners, and the reservation tray.
- **Pill Specialization**: Status indicators ("Mesa Libre", "Sin Gluten") and floating navigation pills retain complete circular rounding (`9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Solar Yellow (`#FFCC00`) background with dark carbon text (`#080A0F`), font weight `700`, `rounded-lg`. Hover brings subtle scaling (1.02x) and a warm glow (`0 4px 20px rgba(255, 204, 0, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Tricolor Ghost)**: Transparent background, 1px border styled with kinetic gradient or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Link**: Solar Yellow text, no border, subtle underline animation on hover.

### Filterable Menu Chips & Tabs
- Horizontal scroll container with hidden scrollbars for mobile ergonomics.
- **Inactive**: `rgba(24, 30, 44, 0.6)` surface, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.7)`.
- **Active**: Filled with Royal Caribbean Blue (`#0033A0`) or Solar Yellow (`#FFCC00`) depending on section context, with high-contrast text and a soft underlying halo.

### Gastronomic Cards
- Built with `rgba(24, 30, 44, 0.72)` glassmorphic backgrounds and `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
- Features image containers with `rounded-lg` inset corners, high-contrast price tags in `#FFCC00`, and allergen icons styled in subtle monochrome translucent badges.
- Kinetic border highlight activates on card hover or tap.

### Inputs & Reservation Fields
- Background: `rgba(18, 22, 32, 0.9)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state: Border turns `#FFCC00` accompanied by an ambient box-shadow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 204, 0, 0.2)`).
- Placeholder: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.35)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px x 20px` with `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- Checked: Solar Yellow `#FFCC00` fill with dark carbon check icon / indicator.

### QR Card Module
- Self-contained, elevated glass container designed for tableside digital menu access.
- High-contrast pure-white central canvas for the QR code matrix surrounded by a dark Venezuelan-kinetic frame and crisp instructions ("Escanea para carta y alérgenos").
